BOBUX OBBY

30 stages. 5 worlds. One epic climb. A Roblox obby for keyboard, mouse, touch and gamepad.

Download the Roblox place file

How to play

  1. Download BobuxObby.rbxlx with the button above.
  2. Open it in Roblox Studio and press Play.
  3. To share it, use File > Publish to Roblox.

Roblox games only run inside Roblox, so this page is the download and guide, not the game itself.

🌙 Moon BootsFloat through the air
🛡 ShieldHazards can't hurt you

Controls

Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move, Space to jump, R to respawn, M for stages, T for trails, P for settings.
Touch: thumbstick, jump button and the on-screen buttons.
Gamepad: left stick to move, A to jump.
